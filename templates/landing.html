Transparent ATS Scoring

Unlike platforms that provide arbitrary "magic numbers", ResumeForge AI breaks down your resume's readiness across verifiable dimensions:

  • Keyword Coverage Rate: Exact calculation of matched required vs. preferred keywords extracted from the job description.
  • Section Completeness: Audit of core ATS sections (Contact, Summary, Experience, Education, Skills).
  • Formatting Diagnostics: Identifies overly long bullet points, date formatting discrepancies, and invalid email formats.
